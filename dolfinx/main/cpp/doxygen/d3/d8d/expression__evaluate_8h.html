Detailed Description

Evaluation of a fem::Expression, and interpolation of an Expression into a fem::Function.

These operations sit above fem::Function because evaluating an Expression requires its coefficient data to be packed, which in turn requires a complete fem::Function.

Check that an Expression can be evaluated over entities of a mesh.

Parameters
[in]eExpression to evaluate.
[in]meshMesh that the Expression is evaluated on.
[in]rankRank of the entities array: 1 for cell indices, 2 for (cell, local entity index) pairs.
Exceptions
std::invalid_argumentif mesh has more than one cell type, e was created on another mesh, its entity dimension does not fit mesh or rank, or its argument is on another mesh whose cells are not the entities evaluated over.
